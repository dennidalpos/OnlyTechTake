## OnlyTechTake

**App Name:** OnlyTechTake: Service & Job Orders
**Short Description:** Local-first job management and technical documentation for your business.
**Full Description:**
🚀 **Optimize Your Service Management with OnlyTechTake!**

Do you need a reliable way to manage service orders and document customer goods? OnlyTechTake is the ultimate "local-first" solution to handle the entire lifecycle of your job orders directly from your Android device. Track every intervention, from intake to final delivery, with professional accuracy.

OnlyTechTake is designed for technical assistance centers, repair labs, and any professional business that requires documented proof of the state of goods and signed intake forms.

🔒 **Total Privacy and Security**
All your data stays exclusively on your device. With an SQLCipher encrypted database and an offline-first philosophy, your customers' information and your business data are protected and always accessible, even without an internet connection. No cloud, no tracking, no leaks.

✨ **Key Features:**
- 📋 **Advanced Job Tracking:** Manage every phase (Open, Diagnosis, In Progress, Ready, Closed) with ease.
- 👥 **Local CRM Database:** Manage contacts, privacy preferences, and intervention history in a secure local archive.
- ✍️ **Integrated Digital Signature:** Capture signatures for terms and privacy acceptance directly on the screen.
- 📸 **Technical Documentation:** Attach photos and videos of goods to provide immutable evidence and help reduce disputes.
- 💬 **Manual WhatsApp handoff:** Prepare customer messages that the operator reviews and sends manually.
- 📄 **Professional PDF Reports:** Create intake forms and status receipts with your own business logo.
- 📊 **XLSX Export & Backup:** Export data for accounting and create encrypted ZIP backups.

**Why choose OnlyTechTake:**
1. **Operational Speed:** A guided workflow designed to make intake and tracking seamless.
2. **Privacy First:** Your business data belongs to you. No cloud subscriptions or third-party servers.
3. **Clear Communication:** Professional message templates for every status update.
4. **Professional Image:** Provide your customers with signed receipts and PDF reports.

Transform your workflow today with OnlyTechTake!
